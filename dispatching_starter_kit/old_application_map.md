Every route registered by the old dispatching module, where it lives, and what it renders.
Generated by walking `~/REPOS/asset_management/app/presentation/routes/dispatching/` for
`@dispatching_bp.route(...)` / `@widgets_bp.route(...)` decorators and their `render_template(...)`
calls. Companion to [initial_prompt.md](initial_prompt.md) (legacy repo navigation) — this is the
literal route → source → template index that document doesn't give you.

**Old stack:** Flask, not Django. Routes are `@blueprint.route(...)` decorators, not `urls.py`. The
"endpoint name" column is the Flask endpoint (`blueprint_name.function_name`), i.e. what
`url_for(...)` takes — the closest legacy equivalent of a Django URL name.

**Mount points** (from `app/presentation/routes/__init__.py`):
- `dispatching_bp` → `/dispatching`
- `dispatching_widgets_bp` → `/dispatching/widgets`

All paths below are relative to those prefixes, e.g. `/requests/new` under `dispatching_bp` is
served at `https://<host>/dispatching/requests/new`.

## Notes for porting

- **No `save`/`create` route ever renders its own success page** — every write handler either
  redirects to a list/detail route or (for HTMX fragments) returns a partial. The "none — redirect"
  and "none — JSON response" rows above are exhaustive, not omissions.
- `dispatching.outcome_create` is a router, not a real form for the dispatch case — it special-cases
  `outcome_type == 'dispatch'` and hands off to `dispatch_create`. Contract/reimbursement/reject
  outcomes share one generic form-rendering path keyed by `_get_outcome_plural()`.
- `/user-portal` is a dead URL kept only as a 302 to `/my-requests` — don't carry the two-URL split
  forward.
- Card templates (`*_card.html` under `outcomes/`) and the `_filter_*_results.html` /
  `*_results.html` fragments are HTMX partials, not full pages — they have no route of their own in
  the new app's page inventory, only in its fragment inventory.
