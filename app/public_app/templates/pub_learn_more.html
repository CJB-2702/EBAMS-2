System Overview

EBAMS is a comprehensive fleet and asset management system designed to streamline operations and simplify tasks. Built on an event-driven architecture, EBAMS tracks all changes and activities across your asset fleet, providing complete visibility and control.

The system is organized into integrated modules that work together to provide a complete solution for managing assets, maintenance workflows, inventory, dispatching, and operational planning.

System Architecture

EBAMS follows a layered architecture pattern for maintainability and scalability:

table_chart Models

Django models defining database schema, organized by application domain (assets, events, parts, administration). Schema and constraints only — no business logic.

settings Control Layer

Adapters, domain structs, factories, handlers, and validators. Implements business rules, writes, and orchestration between models.

travel_explore Presentation Layer

Django views (entrypoints), search, and server-rendered templates. Handles all reads and user interaction.

security Authorization

Django's permission system combined with row-level ownership-group scoping, applied consistently across every sub-application.
