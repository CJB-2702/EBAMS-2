A card is red and read-only when its package is Delivered to Local / Accepted, OR when it carries a split — either condition alone. Lost is NOT a lock trigger. Locked cards still render and their lines still count toward "already packaged", because a Buyer who cannot see the boxes they may not edit will cheerfully plan the same units twice. {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
