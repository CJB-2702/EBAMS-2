{% comment %} D28's allocation-cap decision — ONE component, three call sites (the wizard's per-line allocation card, PO detail's inline allocation editor, and Edit & Linkage's right column). Do not fork it per page. THE CAP IS PER DEMAND, NEVER PER LINE. A PO line is always free to carry more ordered quantity than the sum of its allocations — that is proactive restocking, a vendor minimum order quantity, and reserve for a demand that does not exist yet, all at once (D14/D28). What is capped is how much of a *demand* can be claimed as on order. Exceeding it is neither a silent success nor a flat refusal. It is a choice, and this dialog is where the Buyer makes it: raise the request the demand's quantity_requested moves up to cover the allocation. This edits somebody else's record, so it carries a strong warning. leave excess unallocated allocate only the outstanding amount; the rest of the line stays unclaimed, which is a legitimate line. A modal is correct here despite the "assignment never lives in a modal" rule: this is a one-shot confirmation on a single pending allocation, not the assignment interaction itself — modals.md's single-field-capture / confirmation carve-out, not its excluded case.
