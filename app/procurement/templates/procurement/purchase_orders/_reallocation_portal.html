{% comment %} THE REALLOCATION PORTAL (Demand↔PO Domain) — reallocation_resolution_portal.md §5, §6, §7.8. `# DELIBERATE ANTI-PATTERN`: this is the platform's one sanctioned exception to "assignment never lives in a modal" (see harness/UX_UI/design_patterns/modals.md). Its entire job is to interrupt and force a resolution before the order line's quantity change can save — that is exactly what a modal is for and an in-page flow is poor at. Do not "fix" this into the usual left-heavy in-page pattern. Context: `portal` (dict from `_build_portal_view`) — None unless a reallocation draft is in progress for the currently-selected line. Two dialogs, deliberately two: the Portal itself, and — only when the Buyer has attempted to unlock a LOCKED claim and not yet answered the warning — a second, smaller confirmation dialog stacked on top of it (§6's two-popup unlock sequence). Both are opened here by the server, the same server-decided-open pattern as `_cap_decision_modal.html`, because the server — not a client click — is what decided a decision is owed. {% endcomment %} {% if portal %}
